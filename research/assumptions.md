# Research Assumptions and Uncertainties

## Project Context
The analysis considers a hypothetical Chinese manufacturer evaluating expansion into Nigeria or Kenya.

## Initial Working Assumptions

| ID | Assumption | Status |
|---|---|---|
| A01 | The manufacturer can supply smart security/IoT hardware internationally. | Unverified |
| A02 | Small businesses and property managers are potential customer segments. | Hypothesis |
| A03 | Power reliability and connectivity may influence product selection. | To be tested |
| A04 | Local distributors and installers may provide viable market-entry channels. | To be tested |
| A05 | Product pricing and after-sales support may influence purchasing decisions. | To be tested |

## Important Rules

- Do not treat assumptions as verified facts.
- Update assumptions when new evidence becomes available.
- Document the basis for financial estimates.
- Keep Nigeria and Kenya under consideration until the comparative evaluation is complete.
- Record uncertainty when data is unavailable.

## Open Questions

1. Which market has stronger evidence of paying customers?
2. Which customer segment has the most urgent security needs?
3. What are the prevailing prices for comparable products?
4. Which products are technically suitable for each market?
5. What are the import and regulatory requirements?
6. Which entry strategy could be commercially sustainable?
