# Research Methodology

## Project
Smart Security & IoT Market Entry Analysis: Nigeria vs Kenya

## Research Objectives
This project evaluates Nigeria and Kenya as potential entry markets for a hypothetical Chinese smart security and IoT equipment manufacturer.

The research will assess market demand, customer segments, competitors, product suitability, regulatory requirements, commercial feasibility, and market-entry risks.

## Research Standards

1. **Source quality:** Prioritize government publications, regulatory agencies, official statistics, primary company documents, and reputable industry research.
2. **Source tracking:** Record the source name, original URL, publication date, reference period, and date accessed for important findings.
3. **Evidence classification:** Clearly distinguish verified facts, third-party estimates, assumptions, hypotheses, and illustrative scenarios.
4. **Comparability:** Use equivalent indicators and reference periods for Nigeria and Kenya wherever possible. Document inconsistencies.
5. **Missing information:** Never fabricate missing data. Identify gaps and use explicitly labeled assumptions or scenarios when appropriate.
6. **Reproducibility:** Explain calculations, formulas, currencies, conversion dates, and scoring methods so others can reproduce the analysis.
7. **Conflicting evidence:** Compare source definitions, dates, and methodology. Explain which source is selected and why.
8. **Research limitations:** Disclose unavailable market data, potential selection bias, uncertain estimates, and limitations of public product listings.

## Analytical Approach

1. Conduct country-level market research.
2. Define potential customer segments.
3. Collect and compare competing product listings.
4. Evaluate technical product requirements.
5. Estimate costs and commercial viability.
6. Assess regulatory and operational risks.
7. Develop a weighted market-selection scorecard.
8. Recommend a market-entry strategy based on the available evidence.

## Research Integrity

This is an independent portfolio research project. The company is hypothetical. No primary research, supplier quotation, or market validation will be claimed unless actually conducted.

All research conclusions must be supported by documented evidence or clearly identified assumptions.
