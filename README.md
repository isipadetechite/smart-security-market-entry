# Smart Security & IoT Market Entry Feasibility Study

**Market Intelligence | Business Analytics | Strategic Decision-Making**

## Project Overview

This project evaluates the commercial feasibility of launching smart security and Internet of Things (IoT) solutions in selected emerging markets, initially focusing on **Nigeria and Kenya**.

The study takes the perspective of a hypothetical technology company exploring international market expansion. It combines market research, competitor benchmarking, financial modeling, and business analytics to identify an attractive initial market and recommend an evidence-based entry strategy.

The project is designed to bridge engineering knowledge with commercial decision-making.

## Business Problem

A hypothetical smart security and IoT company is considering expansion into African markets but needs to determine:

- Which market offers the strongest commercially viable entry opportunity?
- Which customer segments have the greatest potential?
- How competitive and affordable are existing solutions?
- What costs, risks, and regulatory barriers could affect entry?
- Under what conditions would launching the business become financially sustainable?

## Research Objectives

1. Estimate the addressable market for selected smart security solutions.
2. Compare Nigeria and Kenya using consistent evaluation criteria.
3. Analyze existing competitors, product offerings, pricing, and distribution channels.
4. Assess customer needs, purchasing constraints, and market barriers.
5. Develop a scenario-based financial feasibility model.
6. Recommend a market entry strategy supported by documented evidence.

## Research Scope

**Geographic markets:** Nigeria and Kenya

**Industry:** Smart Security and Internet of Things (IoT)

**Potential solution categories:**
- Smart surveillance and IP cameras
- Connected intrusion detection and alarm systems
- Remote monitoring and notification solutions
- IoT-based security services for households and small businesses

The final product segment and target customer profile will be refined through the research.

## Analytical Methods

- Secondary market research
- Market segmentation and TAM/SAM/SOM estimation
- Competitor benchmarking
- Weighted market attractiveness scoring
- Scenario and sensitivity analysis
- Unit economics and break-even modeling
- Risk assessment and strategic recommendation
